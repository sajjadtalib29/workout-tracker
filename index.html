🛡️ CSU-Safe Gym & Cutting Protocol

  • 1. Prevent Heat Spikes: Rest 2:30–3:00 between sets and replace speed-10 sprints with steady Incline Walks (Speed 4.5, Incline 12–15).
  • 2. Prevent Pressure Hives: Place a soft cotton towel on benches and machine pads; wear loose breathable cotton clothes.
  • 3. Sweat & Shower: Gently pat sweat dry during lifts. Take a cool/lukewarm shower immediately post-gym and apply Lubrex or Ad.Lib.
  • 4. Cutting Targets (92 kg): ~1,950 kcal/day with 140–160g protein (plain/grilled foods, zero deep-fried or spicy triggers).
